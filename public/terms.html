Terms of service

Last updated 9 September 2026

SeerrSense is a small, self-operated service that lets an AI assistant search and request media through an Overseerr or Jellyseerr instance you control. Using it means accepting what follows.

What the service is

  • It is a layer in front of your Seerr. It stores no media, distributes no media, and makes no content available on its own.
  • Everything it does happens through your instance, with credentials you supply, and is subject to whatever quotas and approval rules you have configured there.
  • It is offered without charge and without a service level. It may be unavailable, changed, or discontinued.

What is expected of you

  • Connect only an instance you are entitled to use, and only with credentials you are entitled to use.
  • You remain responsible for what your Seerr does with the requests it receives, including whether you hold the rights to the content it obtains. This service does not assess that and cannot.
  • Do not use it to attack, overload, or probe anything, including your own instance or this server.
  • Keep your Google account secure. Anyone who can sign in as you can act as you here.

Access

Access is granted to specific accounts and can be withdrawn at any time, without notice, for any reason — most likely because the arrangement that made you a user ended, or because something is being misused. You can end it yourself at any time by disconnecting on your account page and removing the connector from your assistant.

What is not promised

The service is provided as it is. There is no warranty that it will be available, correct, or fit for anything in particular, and no liability is accepted for loss arising from using it or being unable to. In plain terms: a request may fail, a title may be resolved wrongly, and the server may be down. Do not build anything on it that cannot tolerate that.

An assistant acts on your behalf

An AI assistant may call this service on your instruction. It can search freely; requesting media requires a separate permission, and the server refuses to request anything already available or pending. Even so, a model can be mistaken or be led astray by text it reads, so treat requests it makes as your own and check them if it matters.

Changes

These terms may change. The date at the top says when they last did, and the history is public in the repository.
