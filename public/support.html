Getting help

SeerrSense is run by one person. Write and you will get an answer from the person who runs the server and wrote the code, usually within a couple of days.

Check first

  • “No Seerr is connected to this account yet.” Open /account and attach your Overseerr or Jellyseerr. The page tests the address and API key before saving them.
  • “Could not reach your Seerr.” The address on /account must be reachable from the internet. A Seerr behind Cloudflare Access needs its service token in the two Zero Trust fields.
  • “That title is already available / pending.” Working as intended: a request is filed only for something your Seerr does not already have or expect.
  • Sign-in refused. The Google account you chose is not admitted to this server. Try the account you meant, or run your own copy.
  • Reconnecting in the assistant. Remove the SeerrSense connector and add https://seerrsense.mctl.ai/mcp again; the sign-in runs once more.

Where to write

  • Bugs and feature requests: GitHub Issues. Public, searchable, and where fixes are tracked.
  • Anything about your account or your data, including deletion: mashkoffdmitry@gmail.com.

What to include

  1. Which assistant (claude.ai, Claude Code, ChatGPT, other) and roughly when it happened.
  2. What you asked for and what the assistant said back, word for word if you can.
  3. Whether /account shows your Seerr as connected.
  4. Never your API key, your service token, or a password. Nobody here needs them.

Your data

You can remove everything this server holds about you yourself: /account, then Delete my account. What is stored, and for how long, is on the privacy page.
